Important notice

If there is a genuine threat to life or a significant safety risk, a telephone call must be made to the Response Desk immediately. Failure to notify the Response Desk in such circumstances may result in delays to the appropriate emergency response. Our Contractors will endeavour to accommodate the requested attendance time wherever practicable, however we are no longer able to guarantee an immediate response to all requests. Response times will be prioritised based on operational requirements, resource availability, and the nature of the incident. Any misuse of this process, including the submission of non-genuine, false, or misleading requests, will be treated as a serious breach and may result in further action.
