This form is to apply for a switch out of a permanent traffic signal junction or pedestrian crossing located within a London Borough.

If you require multiple locations you will need to complete a separate application for each set of traffic signals.

Before you start

  • DO NOT use this form if you need to amend an existing switch-out, change dates, cancel a switch-out, or apply for a partial switch-out. These requests must be submitted by email to TSSO@tfl.gov.uk.
  • This excludes zebra crossings
  • TfL Traffic Signal Switch Out Guidance and Terms & Conditions
  • TfL Traffic Signal Switch Out Fee structure

You will need:

  • Traffic Management plan with Stage Diagrams
  • Temporary signal timings
  • Works permit - you must gain approval from the relevant Highway Authority prior to submitting a request
