This form is to apply for switch out of a permanent traffic signal junction or pedestrian crossing located within a London Borough.

If you require multiple locations you will need to complete a separate application for each set of traffic signals.

Before you start

  • This application is for a full switch out of traffic signals, if you require a partial switch out please contact TSSO@tfl.gov.uk
  • This excludes zebra crossings
  • TfL Traffic Signal Switch out terms & conditions
  • TfL Traffic Signal Switch out fee structure

You will need:

  • Traffic Management plan with Stage Diagrams
  • Temporary signal timings
  • Works permit - you must gain approval from the relevant Highway Authority prior to submitting a request
