Booking a bulky waste collection takes around 10 minutes.

Bookings cannot be refunded once confirmed.

Before you start

Please read the bulky waste collection page on the council’s website.

How to book

You will need to:

  1. Enter your name, email address and telephone number.
  2. Choose a collection date.
  3. Tell us what you want us to collect. You can also add a description and upload photos of each item.
  4. Tell us where to collect the items from.
  5. Pay online by card, if there is a charge.
