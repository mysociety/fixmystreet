Frequently Asked Questions

What is this site for?

The Boater App helps you tell us about faults affecting navigation or structures on our waterways. You can locate an issue on the map, see whether it has already been reported, and follow reports for updates.

This site is not a navigation or journey-planning tool. It does not show current stoppages or restrictions. Before you travel, check our Notices and stoppages page for the latest information.

What can I report on this site?

Use this site to report non-urgent faults on our network that affect navigation or waterway structures. This includes:

  • broken lock paddles
  • customer service facilities that are broken or not working, including water points, Elsan and pump-outs
  • moving bridges that are not working as they should
  • problems with vegetation

Please choose the closest issue type when you make your report. The site will ask for the details we need.

What should I not report on this site?

Do not use this site for:

  • an urgent or emergency situation
  • a fallen tree that’s blocking the navigation
  • welfare support
  • licensing, waterside mooring or winter mooring enquiries
  • complaints
  • suspected criminal activity
  • general enquiries that are not about a fault on our network

For these matters, please use our contact page. If there is an immediate risk to life, call 999.

How do I report a fallen tree blocking navigation?

Please do not use this site to report a new fallen tree that is blocking navigation. Contact us instead so that we can record the location accurately and assess how urgently action is needed.

You may see fallen-tree reports on the site. These have been added by us after someone contacted us. If the tree has already been reported, you can follow the report to receive any updates. If you are unsure, please contact us.

How do I use the site?

First, tell us where the fault is. You can use your current location, search for a place, or upload a photograph that contains location data.

The map will show reports already made nearby. If the issue is already listed, you can follow it for updates. If it is not listed, select the type of issue, add the requested information and, if helpful, upload photographs.

Will my report be public?

Yes. Your description and photographs will be visible to everyone using the site. Do not include personal information such as your name, contact details, boat index number or details about another person. Only share information and images that are relevant to the fault.

What happens after I submit a report?

We receive every report submitted through the site. We assess it and decide what action is needed using our existing processes. We prioritise work according to factors including condition data from our own inspections, safety, the effect on navigation and customers, available resources and funding. This means we may not be able to resolve every issue immediately.

Why have I not received an update?

You can follow an existing report to receive any status updates we publish. We will provide updates for reports about lock paddles, moving bridges, and customer service facilities. Where possible, we will also update other reports, but we cannot guarantee an individual update for every report or fault type.

A lack of an update does not mean that we have not received your report. Some faults take time to assess, plan, fund, and repair.

Is this a new platform?

We have worked with mySociety to adapt its tried-and-tested FixMyStreet platform for our waterways. Using an established platform has enabled us to offer a clearer way to report faults, see reports made by others, and follow updates.

The platform was originally designed for local authority services, so some features may feel different to a service built only for waterways. We will use your feedback and experience to improve it over time.

Can I use the platform on my phone or tablet?

Yes. The platform is fully responsive and works in any modern web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. It can also be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA), giving you an app-like experience without needing to download anything from an app store. Once installed, it can be launched directly from your home screen and offers offline functionality to allow you to save draft reports when signal is limited.

See instructions on how to download the app.

Once installed, the platform behaves much like a regular app while remaining automatically up to date through your browser.

Do I have to use this site to report a problem?

No. The site is designed to make reporting easier and lets you see and follow existing reports. If you cannot use a smartphone or computer, you can still report an issue by calling 0303 040 4040, and selecting the option to report an issue.

Do you moderate content?

Reports and updates are submitted by users, so we cannot guarantee that all information is accurate. We may edit or remove content that is inappropriate, contains personal information, or is not relevant to the reported fault.

If you see content that may be inappropriate, please call us on 0303 040 4040 and tell us which report it appears on.

Why can’t I select a waterway or structure?

If you cannot select a waterway or structure, it means it’s not owned by Canal & River Trust. You may be able to report the issue to the relevant local authority through the main FixMyStreet service.
